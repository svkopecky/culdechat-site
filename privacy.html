CulDeChat Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Who we are: Direct Innovations LLC, a Virginia limited liability company ("CulDeChat," "we," "us").

Contact: support@culdechat.app

CulDeChat helps you connect with people who live near you. Our core promise is that other users never see where you live. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

CulDeChat is in beta. Some features mentioned below (for example ads and printed postcards from a mailing service) are marked "when added"; they aren't live yet, and we'll update this policy before they are.

1. What we collect

The app won't let you put the pin on your own home; for a party at home, just write "my place".
Event invites you send (Plus) When you invite family and friends to an event, we keep its name, date and time, the place name, and, if you add them, the address and a map pin. Anyone with the invite link sees the name, date, time and place name. The address and pin are shown only to people who answer. Your guests see each other's names and answers, and you see everyone who answered. You can turn the link off, remove anyone from the list, change the event (people who said Going or Maybe are told what changed), or cancel it. If you add people to your invite list, we keep the names and the phone numbers or emails you enter, only to send them the invite (emails come from us; texts are sent from your own phone) and to show you who answered. If you pick people from your phone's contacts, your contacts stay on your phone: only the people you pick are sent to us.
Answering an invite (in the app or on our website, no account needed) On the invite web page you give a name and your answer, and optionally an email address. If the host put you on their list, your own link already has the name the host entered, and if the host added your email we send you the invite and, if you don't answer, at most one reminder a day when the host asks. The host and the other guests see your name and answer. We use the email only to confirm your answer, remind you before the event, and tell you if the host changes or cancels it; leave the box blank to stop these emails. Your browser keeps a private key to your answer so you can change it later (the link in our emails to you includes it, so don't forward those emails). The key isn't in the invite link itself, so forwarding the invite doesn't share your answer or the address. In the app, opening an invite shows the host that you opened it until you answer.
Outage reports (power or internet out at your home) We record the report for your home's rounded area. Neighbors never see your house or your name: once 2 or more homes in a block (about 700 feet across) report, they see that block shaded on the map and a count of homes. Your report clears when you tap It's back, or after 24 hours.
Street problems (a tree down, flooding, ice, a streetlight out, a blocked road) We keep the spot you put the pin on, what it is, your optional note, and the "Still there" and "It's cleared" taps. Neighbors whose circle covers the spot see it on the map with a count of neighbors, never who reported it. It leaves the map once it's cleared or after 48 hours without a "Still there". The spot has to be inside your circle and can't be on your own home.
Lost pet spots and sightings If you post a lost pet, you can add where it was last seen; neighbors who can see your post can add sightings (a spot on the map and about when). These show on the map of neighbors who can see the post, as a trail. The pet's owner is told who added a sighting, so they can follow up. They leave the map when the pet is marked found, or after 7 days. Spots can't be placed on your own home.
Thank-yous When you thank a neighbor, it's posted as a comment on their post that others in their circle can see; they get a notice, and it counts as a Helpful mark on that post.
Trash and recycling reminders Your neighborhood's pickup days are set by its moderators. We keep whether you want the reminder and the time you chose, and the date we last reminded you so you only get one.
Photos you add to posts and comments (up to 2 per post, 1 per comment) To show them to the neighbors in your circle. Before a photo leaves your phone, the app shrinks it and removes its hidden location and camera details (EXIF data), so a photo can't reveal where it was taken. The app opens your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo, and only the photo you pick is uploaded.
"Want it" votes in What's new When you tap ♥ Want it on a feature that's coming soon, we keep that you want it, so we know what neighbors are most excited about and can build those first. Only the total for each feature is used; it's never shown to other members. Tap it again to take your vote back.
Reports, blocks and moderation decisions To keep the community safe. People you block aren't told. We keep a record of content a moderator removed and any pause on your account, so repeated problems can be handled fairly.
Around Town suggestions To review and publish local events and information you send us. A public event you suggest shows its date, time, place (a public place you pick on the map or with place search, never a home) and your display name to members within about 20 miles of the place, after a moderator checks it. It drops off Around Town when it ends.
Events you mark ★ Interested To remind you before the event and show it in your Events list. Others only see how many people are interested, never who. Tap again to remove it anytime.
Notification settings, alert keywords, and your time zone To send the notifications you asked for (including event reminders at the time you chose), and the daily summary at the time you chose.
Messages you send to support (with your email, display name, app version, and device type) To answer you and fix problems. They're delivered to our support inbox by email.

2. How your location is protected

  • Other users never see your home, your address, or your exact location.
  • To decide whose circles overlap, our servers use your home rounded to an area of about 500 feet, so even someone who keeps shrinking their circle can't pinpoint your house.
  • Your exact location is stored separately and used only by our servers.
  • To manage home codes, the CulDeChat team can see which addresses in a neighborhood have verified and which were mailed a new code. This is never shown to other users.
  • Weather on My Radius is looked up by our servers for your home's area rounded to about 0.6 mile (1 km). Your phone never sends your location for weather, and the weather service never gets your name.
  • Outage reports are shown only as shaded blocks once 2 or more homes report, never as houses.
  • Searching an address in the app never reveals whether a CulDeChat member lives there.

3. How we use information

  • To run CulDeChat: accounts, circles, feeds, messages, notifications, badges, and address verification.
  • To keep people safe: preventing fake accounts and spam, reviewing reports, and enforcing our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  • To answer support messages.
  • To provide subscriptions and, on the Free plan, ads (when added).
  • To improve the app and fix problems.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

Other users see only what you choose to share with your circle: your display name (or alias), badges, posts, comments, photos, and messages. Never your location. Photos are stored privately and can be opened only by neighbors in your circle (and our moderators, when reviewing reports), through links that expire after an hour. The app has no button to download or share another member's photo, but anyone who can see a photo could still take a screenshot, so only post photos you're comfortable sharing with your circle.

5. How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, we keep the information needed to run it.
  • If you delete your account, we delete your profile, homes, posts, comments, messages, and settings right away (and from any backups within 30 days). Photo files from deleted posts, comments, and accounts are removed from storage within a day. Reports other people made about you are kept for safety, without your name attached.
  • If an account was closed because the person is under 18, we keep only a one-way code made from the email address (not the address itself) so the same email can't sign up again.
  • We may keep limited records longer when needed for safety (for example, records of accounts banned for abuse), fraud prevention, or legal requirements.
  • Support emails stay in our support inbox for as long as needed to help you and keep a record of the conversation.
  • Verification codes expire after about 30 days.
  • Event invites, everyone's answers and the emails we sent for them are deleted 30 days after the event ends.
  • Outage reports are deleted after 7 days. Street problem reports and lost pet sightings are deleted 30 days after they leave the map.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account anytime in the app (Account → Delete my account) or by following the steps at culdechat.app/delete-account.
  • Location permission: you can say no and place your home on the map instead.
  • Private profile: hide your name from neighbors (they see an alias) and turn off messages. Or keep your name and just turn off messages.
  • Block anyone; they won't see your posts or be able to message you.
  • Notifications: turn them on or off in the app or your phone's settings.
  • Access or correct your data: contact us at support@culdechat.app.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Virginia, or other US states), you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your data, and to opt out of targeted advertising. We won't treat you differently for using them. Contact support@culdechat.app.

7. Children

CulDeChat is for people 18 and older. We check age at sign-up (and will use the age information Apple and Google provide, where available). We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls in our database so each piece of data is visible only to the people allowed to see it, and limited staff access. No system is perfectly secure; please contact us if you see a problem.

9. Where data is stored

Our servers are in the United States.

We'll post updates here and change the effective date. For significant changes, we'll notify you in the app.
