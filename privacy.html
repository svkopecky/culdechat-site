CulDeChat Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Who we are: Direct Innovations LLC, a Virginia limited liability company ("CulDeChat," "we," "us").

Contact: support@culdechat.app

CulDeChat helps you connect with people who live near you. Our core promise is that other users never see where you live. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

CulDeChat is in beta. Some features mentioned below (for example ads and mailed verification postcards) are marked "when added"; they aren't live yet, and we'll update this policy before they are.

1. What we collect

Information you give us

Before a photo leaves your phone, the app shrinks it and removes its hidden location and camera details (EXIF data), so a photo can't reveal where it was taken. The app opens your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo, and only the photo you pick is uploaded.

2. How your location is protected

  • Other users never see your home, your address, or your exact location.
  • To decide whose circles overlap, our servers use your home rounded to an area of about 500 feet, so even someone who keeps shrinking their circle can't pinpoint your house.
  • Your exact location is stored separately and used only by our servers.
  • Searching an address in the app never reveals whether a CulDeChat member lives there.

3. How we use information

  • To run CulDeChat: accounts, circles, feeds, messages, notifications, badges, and address verification.
  • To keep people safe: preventing fake accounts and spam, reviewing reports, and enforcing our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  • To answer support messages.
  • To provide subscriptions and, on the Free plan, ads (when added).
  • To improve the app and fix problems.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

Other users see only what you choose to share with your circle: your display name (or alias), badges, posts, comments, photos, and messages. Never your location. Photos are stored privately and can be opened only by neighbors in your circle (and our moderators, when reviewing reports), through links that expire after an hour. The app has no button to download or share another member's photo, but anyone who can see a photo could still take a screenshot, so only post photos you're comfortable sharing with your circle.

5. How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, we keep the information needed to run it.
  • If you delete your account, we delete your profile, homes, posts, comments, messages, and settings right away (and from any backups within 30 days). Photo files from deleted posts, comments, and accounts are removed from storage within a day. Reports other people made about you are kept for safety, without your name attached.
  • If an account was closed because the person is under 18, we keep only a one-way code made from the email address (not the address itself) so the same email can't sign up again.
  • We may keep limited records longer when needed for safety (for example, records of accounts banned for abuse), fraud prevention, or legal requirements.
  • Support emails stay in our support inbox for as long as needed to help you and keep a record of the conversation.
  • Verification codes expire after about 30 days.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account anytime in the app (Account → Delete my account) or by following the steps at culdechat.app/delete-account.
  • Location permission: you can say no and place your home on the map instead.
  • Private profile: hide your name from neighbors (they see an alias) and turn off messages. Or keep your name and just turn off messages.
  • Block anyone; they won't see your posts or be able to message you.
  • Notifications: turn them on or off in the app or your phone's settings.
  • Access or correct your data: contact us at support@culdechat.app.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Virginia, or other US states), you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your data, and to opt out of targeted advertising. We won't treat you differently for using them. Contact support@culdechat.app.

7. Children

CulDeChat is for people 18 and older. We check age at sign-up (and will use the age information Apple and Google provide, where available). We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls in our database so each piece of data is visible only to the people allowed to see it, and limited staff access. No system is perfectly secure; please contact us if you see a problem.

9. Where data is stored

Our servers are in the United States.

10. Changes

We'll post updates here and change the effective date. For significant changes, we'll notify you in the app.
