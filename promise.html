Our privacy promise

CulDeChat is for talking with the people who live around you, without telling the world where you live. These are the rules we build by.

  1. We never show where you live. Not your address, not your house on a map, not your exact location. Neighbors see your name and what you post, and that's it. Our servers work with your home rounded to an area about 500 feet across, so nobody can narrow it down to your house.
  2. Every neighbor is a real, verified home. Every home is verified with a code delivered to that address, so the people in your circle really live around you, not strangers from across town or bots.
  3. We never sell your information. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.
  4. Ads never follow you. If the free plan shows ads, they'll be about your area, never built from what you read, post or do.
  5. Your private things stay private. Messages are between you and the person you're writing to. Your close neighbors list is yours alone, and nobody is told when you add or remove them.
  6. Maps show areas and streets, never houses. Outages show as shaded blocks once 2 homes report them. Holiday lights show by street once 2 homes there are lit. Your own home is only ever shown to you.
  7. You're in control. Make your profile private, turn off messages, block anyone, and delete your account and everything in it whenever you want.

What we're honest about

A promise only works if it's straight about its limits.

  • Our small team can see which addresses have joined, because we deliver the home codes. That list is never shown to neighbors or anyone else, and we never publish it.
  • Moderators can read a post, comment or message that someone reports, so they can keep the neighborhood safe. The app gives them no way to read anyone's messages otherwise.
  • Anything you post, your circle can see and could screenshot. Only share what you're comfortable sharing with your neighbors.
  • We follow the law. If we're legally required to share information, or someone's safety is at risk, we may have to.
  • We use a few trusted services to run the app (hosting, notifications, maps, weather). Each gets only what it needs, and none of them gets your exact location. The full list is in our Privacy Policy.
